Our Story

Local care you can feel confident leaving your dog with.

Room & Groom is based in Ballygarrett, Co. Wexford and provides dog boarding, daycare and professional grooming.

The focus is simple: a safe, caring and welcoming environment where every dog is treated as an individual.

Whether your dog is staying overnight, spending the day with us or visiting for grooming, the aim is to make their experience as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

What Matters To Us

Safe care. Individual attention. Happy dogs.

Safety

A secure environment is at the heart of everything we do, from boarding to daycare and grooming.

Comfort

Every dog has different needs, routines and personalities, so care should never feel one-size-fits-all.

Trust

Leaving your dog with someone else is a big decision. We want owners to feel informed, reassured and confident.
